# aqi-anomaly-dashboard: interface design

Reference: [mintlify design analysis](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/mintlify/DESIGN.md), curated by VoltAgent. This is an independent adaptation; no affiliation, brand assets or proprietary fonts.

Single light reading workspace. Mint active states, white surfaces and a fixed palette (see `web/src/styles/tokens.css`; new tokens may only alias existing values).

2026-11 relayout, "weather app × lab notebook": the home page opens on a "now → next 24 hours" board (one AQI-coloured hour strip per location, P50 fill with a P90 top stripe). Analysis pages read as numbered chapters: a one-sentence takeaway computed from the data, then the chart, then a "how to read" margin note. Navigation is a 248px notebook index rail at 1200px and wider, a sticky top bar from 641px to 1199px (chapters beside the brand from 1100px, on their own row below that, so all six always fit), and a bottom tab bar on phones.

2026-11 refinement pass (palette unchanged): one chip metric for eyebrows, badges, tags and AQI chips (24px high, 13px bold, tabular figures); one motion vocabulary (`--duration` / `--ease`, finite transitions only, zeroed under reduced motion); hairline gridlines with one firmer baseline and short tick marks; axis ticks chosen from the chart's measured width (`web/src/lib/chart-layout.ts`), so phones get date-only labels at midnight; the P10–P90 band has hairline edges and the forecast side of the chart a faint tint; map labels are placed by a small collision search and drawn at a fixed on-screen size; empty, loading and error states share one card shape with a marker.

2026-11 documentation-grade pass (palette unchanged). 參考 awesome-design-md（MIT）的 Mintlify DESIGN.md 版面與字體規則，配色維持本站. One idea, the section wash, comes from the same collection's NerdWallet analysis. Only non-colour rules were adopted; the reference colours, fonts, logos and wording were not. New tokens in `tokens.css` are spacing, radius and type values or aliases of existing colours (`--wash` = `--bg-sunken`, `--tile` = `--accent-soft`, `--tile-ink` = `--accent`); no colour value changed and no colour literal was added.

- Reading type: 16px body at 1.7 line height, page briefs and ledes at 1.8; prose, takeaways and briefs share one measure of 40em (about 40 CJK characters per line); chapter takeaways are an 18px subtitle rather than a second heading; headings at 1.3 line height (h1 1.2), negative tracking on h1 only.
- Spacing: 4px base. 48px between overview bands (`--gap-band`), 32px between analysis sections (`--gap-section`), 24px card padding (`--pad-card`), 12/16px table cells (`--pad-row`).
- Radius scale: 4px code chips, 6px nav rows, 8px controls, inputs, notes and table frames, 12px cards, 16px for the one feature panel per page (the 24-hour board, the what-if reading, the overview's chapter-index wash), pills for buttons, toggles, badges and chips.
- Elevation restraint: cards, KPI rows, state cards and the station "now" panel rest flat on a hairline border. Only hovered links take the faint `--shadow`, and only floating chrome (the phone tab bar, the sticky replay controls) keeps a stronger one. The one exception is the overview's linked cards (insight cards and chapter cards): on hover-capable devices they lift 2px with a soft shadow, so they read as something to open.
- Sparing accent: the accent marks the active chapter (a 3px rail edge, a 2px underline in the top bar, the tab-bar marker), links, takeaway figures, page icons and the chapter number tile. "How to read" notes and the chapter index are neutral; an insight card that names one station's level carries that AQI band as a 4px top rule (the chip inside still states the label).
- Tabs: radio filters (who is going out, local or regional) are a toggle: pill options inside one washed track, the chosen option filled ink; 36px tall on desktop, 44px on phones.
- Data tables: 14px cells, header row on the wash, hairline row rules and no vertical rules, numbers right-aligned in tabular figures; a table outside a card gets its own 8px hairline frame.
- Icon tiles and section wash: chapter numbers inside an analysis page sit on a soft tint square (32px); the overview's chapter index shows each page's icon on a 40px tint square and sits on a pale wash panel holding white cards, instead of a bordered box.
- Page icons: one line icon per page (`web/src/components/pageIcons.ts`), shared by the rail, the top bar, the phone tab bar and the overview's chapter cards. Drawn in-house on a 24px grid with a 2px round stroke and `currentColor` (`web/src/components/Icon.tsx`), inline SVG so they stay inside the CSP and cost no request. Always decorative (`aria-hidden`): the text next to an icon carries the meaning. Smaller icons also lead card labels, secondary buttons, the data badge and the error and empty states. Nav order: overview, propagation, anomalies, what-if, model, planner, data trust.
- Long lists that scroll inside a card (the anomaly event list on wide screens) fade their edge only where more items are hidden, using a scroll-driven mask; browsers without scroll-driven animations keep the plain list.

Use local system fonts (each platform's built-in CJK face first) in two weights only, 400 and 700, 16px body and 14px secondary text. No entrance animation on page content. Keep semantic data colors distinct from decorative accents. Preserve data labels, warnings, keyboard focus, chart tables, cancellation and existing route contracts. No external font or asset requests. Mobile controls retain at least 44px height. All pages reflow at 200% text; wide data tables scroll inside named keyboard-accessible regions.
